Collapse Challenge

A new quantum puzzle every day. Measuring a qubit makes it collapse: the blend turns into a plain 0 or 1. Build the target state with as few gates as you can, and match it before you measure.

    How to play

    Four steps

    1. Look at the Start and the Target. They show a qubit before anyone measures it.
    2. Press gates to build a list. Your state card changes after every gate.
    3. Press Check when you think your state matches the Target. The bars and the dial both have to match.
    4. Use fewer gates for more stars. Par is the fewest gates that can do the job.

    New to qubits? The Quantum Gates lesson covers X, H and Z. The Y gate is explained in the puzzle where it first appears.

    What am I looking at?

    • The dial. The arrow shows which way the qubit points. Top is |0⟩, bottom is |1⟩, right is |+⟩ and left is |−⟩. The right and left sides give the same bars but are different states. That difference is a hidden sign.
    • The bars. They show your chance of getting 0 or 1 if you measured right now.
    • The amplitudes. These numbers are the state itself, before measuring. Squaring them gives the bars, but a minus sign hides in the squaring.

    Remember: 0 and 1 are what you get after you measure. The amplitudes show the state before measuring. Peek measures once and collapses your qubit into a plain 0 or 1. In real life you cannot undo a measurement, so here Undo cannot take a peek back: Reset starts a new attempt. Match the Target before you measure. A peek costs one star.
